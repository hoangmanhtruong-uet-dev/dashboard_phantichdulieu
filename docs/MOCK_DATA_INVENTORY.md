# Remaining mock data inventory

The canonical, exact Phase 4 inventory is maintained in
[`PHASE4_API_INVENTORY.md`](PHASE4_API_INVENTORY.md).

Remaining synthetic analytics are limited to the order-derived funnel, the
deterministic cohort matrix, and development seed records enabled explicitly by
`SEED_DEMO_DATA=true`. Unsupported UI capabilities show `BLOCKED` and do not
pretend to save, export, notify, forecast or manage settings.
